## Method

- Gold SQL is written against the **base tables** (`customers`, `orders`,
  `products`, `categories`, `order_items`, `payments`, `reviews`), not the
  `analytics_*` views, and is executed on a privileged connection that bypasses
  the guardrails. Every tenant-scoped gold filters `tenant_id` explicitly.
- Each ALLOW record carries two formulations: `A` (direct joins + GROUP BY, in
  `<id>.sql`) and `B` (a different shape — CTE, window, EXISTS, subquery, set
  operation — in `<id>.b.sql`). Agreement is machine-proven here, never asserted.
- Relative time is anchored to one injected `EVAL_NOW` via the `:eval_now` token;
  the `:tenant` token is substituted per record. No gold may call `now()`,
  `CURRENT_DATE`, `random()`, or any other unstable function.

> Authorship caveat (R3): both formulations were authored from the question and
> schema, A before B, but by the same authoring session. The independent-review
> control is the machine agreement check plus structural difference, not literal
> author isolation; a second human/model pass over `.b.sql` is a good next step.
